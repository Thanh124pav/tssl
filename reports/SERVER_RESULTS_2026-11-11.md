# Hub / local / decenter / soft-target arms on the H100 server: 3-seed results

**Date:** 2026-11-11. Server runs (Slurm, 1× H100, env `acvcl`), follow-up to `METHOD_STATUS_2026-11-11.md`.
**One line:** no arm improves segmentation and alignment together. `hub` is the only source of segmentation gain and it lowers Kendall τ on every benchmark; `local` is the only source of AP@K gain; blurring the alignment target is the only source of τ gain. Decentering (with or without `local`) collapses on all five YTI activities.

## 1. Protocol

- 7 benchmarks: FSeval, Desktop Assembly (DA), YTI × {changing_tire, coffee, cpr, jump_car, repot}; published recipe per benchmark (`scripts/run_mrt_matrix.sh`).
- 3 seeds (0, 1, 2) for every arm and benchmark below; 357 training runs, 0 failures.
- Segmentation: MoF / F1 / mIoU from the training script's full-video test. Alignment: `scripts/eval_va.py` on `last.ckpt` (SVM accuracy, Kendall τ, AP@5/10/15).
- Every number in §3 is the **paired difference to the baseline of the same seed**, averaged over seeds and then over the 7 benchmarks; in parentheses, on how many of the 7 benchmarks the difference is positive. Per-benchmark tables are in the appendix.
- Pouring and Penn Action are not included: the data is not on the server (Drive is not reachable from the compute nodes).

## 2. Arms

All are flags of `repos/VASOT/src/train.py`; arms not listed here are defined in `METHOD_STATUS_2026-11-11.md`.

| Arm | Flags | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `dc` | `--decenter 1.0` | subtract the EMA embedding mean before every cosine cost/logit |
| `dc_local` | `dc` + `--local-weight 1.0` | global decenter + intra-action local loss |
| `blurW` (W = 3, 6, 12) | `--align-target-blur W` | box-blur the alignment target `T` over ±W frames, row mass preserved (softer target) |
| `pow05`, `pow2` | `--align-target-power 0.5 / 2.0` | `T^γ`, rows rescaled to their previous mass (γ<1 softens, γ>1 sharpens). **New flag in this commit.** |
| `local_pow2`, `blur6_local`, `blur12_local` | target change + `--local-weight 1.0` | combinations with the local loss |

## 4. Reading

1. **Hub helps segmentation and harms alignment.** `hub` and `hub_local` gain +2.9 MoF, and τ drops on 7/7 benchmarks. Adding `local` to `hub` recovers part of the alignment loss (AP@5 −2.2 → −0.7, Acc −1.9 → −0.8) and none of the τ loss in sign (−2.8 → −2.0, still 0/7).
2. **Local is small and consistent.** `local` alone: AP@5/10/15 up on 7/7 benchmarks (+0.8 to +0.9), segmentation +0.5, τ unchanged. The normalised residual matters: `local_raw` loses mIoU (−1.3) and τ (−1.2).
3. **Decentering is benchmark-dependent and unsafe.** `dc` improves FSeval (MoF +3.1, 3/3 seeds) and DA (MoF +3.7, 3/3) and collapses on all five YTI activities (MoF −1.6 to −14.6; τ −15 to −30; AP@5 −8 to −17). `dc_local` is indistinguishable from `dc`: the local loss does not rescue it. The cause of the YTI collapse was not investigated.
4. **Softening the target raises τ monotonically with the blur width** (+0.8, +1.9, +3.1 for W = 3, 6, 12; 7/7 benchmarks each) and costs segmentation as W grows (mIoU +0.3, −0.4, −1.0). All blur arms lose about 1.6 MoF on FSeval. Softening by power (`pow05`) does not give the τ gain and loses 4.7 MoF on FSeval (0/3 seeds).
5. **Sharpening (`pow2`) is a small segmentation gain driven by DA** (+5.7 MoF, 3/3 seeds; +0.3 on FSeval, 1/3 seeds) with a slight τ loss (1/7). A seed-0 reading of +6.2 MoF on FSeval was seed noise.
6. **`blur6_local` is the one arm that raises τ and AP@K without losing segmentation** (τ +2.0 on 7/7, AP@5 +0.8 on 6/7, MoF +0.2, F1 +0.7). It has none of the hub's segmentation gain.

Current picture: `hub_local` is the segmentation configuration, `blur6_local` the alignment configuration; no tested arm is both. `hub` + blur was not run (the blur is applied after the hub mixing, on a target that is already block-uniform).

## 5. Soft-target diagnostic

`scripts/diag_soft_target.py` decomposes the alignment loss on frozen checkpoints into `H(T) + KL(T‖P)` for the baseline target and its softened / sharpened versions. On the FSeval baseline checkpoint the model's `P` has a much larger effective support than the OT target `T` (about 128 vs 32 frames): the row-softmax `P` is too flat, not `T` too sharp. The hub target is block-uniform and carries no intra-action ordering. Outputs: `artifacts/runs/current/diag_soft/` (not tracked).
